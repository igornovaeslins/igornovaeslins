#### 📌 Featured: *Criminal Order and Gendered Violence in Chicago*

Women are a rising share of the people shot in Chicago — about one in ten through 2016, nearly **one in six by 2022**. What predicts violence against women is not which gang holds a neighborhood, but whether a stable territorial order exists at all.

#### 🧭 Research

- **Criminal governance & elections** — how militias and drug factions in Rio de Janeiro reorganize voter mobilization, registration, and electoral competition.
- **Criminal order & gender** — how armed territorial control regulates and silences violence against women, and releases it when control collapses.
- **State, police, and crime** — how who the state appoints to command the local police battalion shapes criminal territory and lethal violence.

Methods: difference-in-differences, event study, spatial analysis, quasi-experimental designs — reproducible in R.
